4. Premium Wall Tattoos – Intended Use

Marie's Painted Worlds Premium Wall Tattoos are high-quality decorative wall products and should be handled and installed with care. They are intended primarily to be applied to a suitable wall or surface and left in place as a decorative element.

Although the material may permit careful repositioning during installation and, depending on the surface and circumstances, later removal or reapplication, the products are not designed to be repeatedly moved or treated as reusable children's stickers.

Larger, taller or more intricate motifs can require patience during installation. Installation by two people is recommended where appropriate.

Before installation, the customer should ensure that the intended surface is clean, dry, smooth, stable and suitable for self-adhesive wall decoration. Freshly painted surfaces must be allowed to dry and fully cure in accordance with the paint manufacturer's instructions.

Paint type, wall texture, moisture, dust, surface preparation, age of paint and other factors can affect adhesion and removal. Customers should read and follow the installation instructions supplied with or linked to the product.
